Color Scheme

The chosen colors reflect the vibrant sunsets, deep canyons, and outdoor spirit of Utah:

Primary Color: #3e1383 (Used for Navigation, Headers, and Footer backgrounds)
Accent Color: #2c0066 (Used for Section Headings and borders)
Background Light: #f4eef7 (Used for target audience question card background fills)

Typography

We are using a balanced combination of fonts to ensure readability and personality:

  • Macondo: Applied exclusively to the main page titles (like the Hero `h1`) for an elegant, adventurous look.
  • Comic Relief (or system-ui): Applied to the body text, regular navigation buttons, subtitles, and paragraphs for high accessibility.
